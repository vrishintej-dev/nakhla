Your farm

Measure each water source once a month with a TDS/EC pen (about AED 30). Do a bottle test on one dripper per block: salty water clogs drippers, so the real flow is often lower than the label. Everything saves on this phone.

How Nakhla decides

  1. Palm need = ET0 × Kc 0.95 × canopy area. ET0 is the FAO-56 Penman-Monteith reference evapotranspiration for your farm's coordinates, from Open-Meteo (ERA5 + forecast). The model matches the Environment Agency Abu Dhabi sap-flow study: about 180 L in summer and 60–80 L in winter.
  2. Salt flush: FAO-29 leaching requirement LR = ECw ÷ (5 × ECe − ECw), with the date palm tolerating ECe 4.0 dS/m at full yield. Cutting water on a brackish well without this lets salt build up in the root zone.
  3. Run time = need ÷ drip efficiency ÷ (1 − LR) ÷ emitter flow, rounded up to 5 minutes.
  4. Works offline: the last forecast is stored on the phone, so a missed connection still gives a safe plan.
